# OnePerson Empire Marketing Plan

This plan is for marketing the website builder to early testers and first paying customers. The goal is not scale yet. The goal is to find the customer segment that says, "Yes, I need this now."

## Positioning

OnePerson Empire helps solo/local business owners get a simple website live fast, with editable text/photos, owner payment setup, and a path to a real domain.

Simple promise:

```text
Get a small-business website live today. Edit the words and photos yourself. Start with Venmo, upgrade to Stripe/PayPal and a real domain when ready.
```

## Best Early Customers

Start with businesses where a simple website can directly create trust or bookings:

- Mobile detailers
- Cleaners
- Dog walkers
- Tutors
- Home service providers
- Bakers/cottage food sellers
- Handymen
- Lawn care
- Coaches or consultants
- Small event vendors

Avoid complex ecommerce, restaurants with big menus, regulated industries, or businesses that need scheduling/payments deeply integrated from day one.

## Main Customer Pain

They do not want "a website builder." They want:

- A page that makes them look real.
- A link they can send to customers.
- A way for people to contact or pay them.
- Help with photos, wording, domain, and updates.
- Something less scary than Wix/Squarespace/Webflow.

## Offer For Testers

Use this message:

```text
I'm testing a simple website builder for solo/local businesses.

Can you try it for 20-30 minutes and give me honest feedback?

I'm testing whether it is easy to understand, not whether you are good at using it. I mostly want to know where it feels confusing, risky, or useful.
```

If they say yes:

```text
Great. I'll have you build a simple small-business website, edit one thing, set up payment, publish it, and tell me what you think out loud as you go.
```

## First Marketing Channels

Do not start with ads. Start with direct conversations.

1. Personal network: anyone who runs or knows a tiny local business.
2. Local Facebook groups: ask for feedback, not sales.
3. Reddit/local business communities: careful, non-spammy feedback request.
4. Craigslist/service providers: message a few businesses with weak/no websites.
5. Real-world businesses: cleaners, detailers, tutors, lawn care signs, flyers, vans.

## Outreach Scripts

### Soft Feedback Ask

```text
Hey, I am building a simple website tool for solo/local businesses.

Would you be willing to try it for 20-30 minutes and tell me what is confusing or useful?

No sales pitch. I am trying to learn what small business owners actually need.
```

### Business Owner Ask

```text
I noticed your business could probably use a simple website link for customers.

I'm testing a tool that builds a simple site fast, lets you edit the words/photos, and helps set up Venmo or a checkout link.

Would you be open to trying it and giving honest feedback?
```

### Done-For-You Angle

```text
I can make you a simple website draft and send you the live link.

If it looks useful, you can tell me what to change. If not, no pressure. I'm using the feedback to improve the product.
```

## Early Content Ideas

Make short posts/videos showing outcomes, not features:

- "Built a dog-walking website in 5 minutes."
- "How a solo cleaner can get a website link today."
- "Venmo first, Stripe later: simple payments for tiny businesses."
- "Why a real photo beats an AI photo for local trust."
- "Share your tester URL first, connect a domain later."

## What To Measure

Track this manually at first:

- How many people agree to test?
- How many complete a session?
- How many publish a site?
- How many would share the link with a customer?
- How many ask for help with domain/payment?
- How many ask, "Can you just do it for me?"
- What price do they expect for setup/help?

## Pricing Hypotheses To Test Later

Do not lead with pricing yet. Listen first.

Possible offers:

- Free tester site while product is early.
- $49-$99 assisted setup.
- $199-$399 done-for-you launch package.
- $15-$39/month for hosting and small updates.
- Paid domain/payment setup help as an add-on.

## 30-Day Plan

### Week 1: Five Feedback Sessions

- Recruit 5 testers.
- Run the feedback process in `tester-feedback/`.
- Record repeated confusion.
- Fix only the top repeated blocker.

### Week 2: Ten More Conversations

- Message 10-20 real local businesses.
- Offer a free draft or feedback session.
- Watch who responds and why.

### Week 3: First Case Studies

- Pick 2-3 decent generated sites.
- Get permission to show before/after or anonymized examples.
- Write short stories: business, problem, site, payment/domain path.

### Week 4: First Paid Offer Test

- Offer a done-for-you setup to the warmest leads.
- Keep the offer simple: website draft, edits, publish link, payment setup, domain guidance.
- Do not scale until at least a few people either pay or clearly say they would pay after specific fixes.

## Security Before Marketing

Before broad public marketing, do the security checklist in `marketing/security-before-marketing.md`.

For the first 5 private testers, you can proceed if you keep the link limited and avoid collecting sensitive credentials. Before posting publicly or inviting strangers at scale, tighten security around publishing, editing, webhooks, invoices, environment secrets, and generated HTML.
