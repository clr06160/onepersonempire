# OnePerson Empire

**AI Co-Founder in a Box** — Powered by Gemini

A multi-agent AI swarm that takes any business idea in plain English and instantly builds, launches, and operates a real revenue-generating micro-business.

**Live entry for Build with Gemini XPRIZE 2026** ($2M total prizes)

### What it does
- You describe your business idea in plain English  
- Gemini agent swarm instantly does:
  - Market validation
  - Full business plan + financial model
  - Auto-generates & deploys a beautiful landing page + Stripe checkout
  - Creates initial marketing assets
  - Runs day-to-day operations with visible logs
- You watch everything live on a dashboard while the AI literally co-founds and runs the business for you.
