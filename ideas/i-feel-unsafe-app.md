# Side Product Idea: I Feel Unsafe App

## One-Line Idea

An app for the moment before someone is ready to call 911: when something feels wrong, it alerts trusted people, shares location, starts an incident timeline, and preserves evidence.

## Why It Might Matter

People often sense danger before they have proof or certainty. In that gap, they may freeze, wait too long, or avoid calling police because they are not sure what is happening yet.

This app is not secret surveillance. It is a user-controlled safety button.

## Core MVP

- Big `I feel unsafe` button.
- Sends live location and timestamp to trusted contacts.
- Starts an incident timeline.
- Starts audio/video/photo capture where legally allowed and clearly consented.
- Uploads evidence to a secure cloud vault immediately.
- Countdown escalation: if the user does not cancel, trusted contacts get stronger alerts.
- `I am safe` closeout flow.
- `Escalate now` emergency option.

## Possible Customers

- Parents of teens.
- College students.
- Women dating, traveling, using rideshare, or walking alone.
- Domestic violence safety organizations.
- Schools, churches, youth groups.
- Field workers and solo workers.

## Positioning

Do not call it "catch predators" or anything vigilante.

Better framing:

- "When something feels wrong, do not be alone."
- "A safety button before it becomes a 911 emergency."
- "Preserve evidence and alert trusted people fast."

## Safety And Legal Constraints

- No secret recording.
- Clear consent and visible safety state.
- Respect local recording laws.
- Strong privacy controls.
- Evidence vault must be secure.
- Emergency escalation should not impersonate law enforcement.
- Serious cases need professional reporting paths, not vigilante action.

## Notes

This should stay separate from OnePerson Empire. It is a high-trust, high-liability product and should not distract from making the builder robust.
