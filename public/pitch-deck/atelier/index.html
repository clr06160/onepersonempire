Benefits you design. Experiences you remember.

A more customizable benefits platform — with exclusive, personalized retreats as the high-emotion layer.

For Edenred & partners Confidential

Why we’re here

A white-space product for platforms that already own the daily perk.

Edenred dominates meal, commute, gift, and lifestyle spend. What’s missing is the high-emotion layer: exclusive, customizable retreats employees actually talk about.

The ask

  • Strategic partnership / pilot with 5–10 enterprise clients
  • Distribution through your employer portal & apps
  • Optional co-brand or regional license

The problem

Benefits feel interchangeable. Engagement still leaks.

Commoditized perks

Meals, commute, and LSAs are table stakes. Employees compare on salary — not another prepaid card.

Retreats are chaos

Offsites live in inboxes. No benefits infra, no usage data, no employee choice, no recurring platform revenue.

One-size fails

Hybrid teams and different energy levels mean a single “fun weekend” alienates half the room.

Strategic insight

The next engagement lever isn’t more merchants — it’s choice of moments.

Edenred’s “Power up your joie de vivre” already points past transactional perks. Personalized retreats are the premium expression of that promise — and Amplify25–28 prioritizes higher ARPU on a modular platform.

Solution

Compose benefits like a studio brief.

  1. ComposeHR picks pillars: lifestyle, wellness, learning, retreats, recognition.
  2. PersonalizeEmployees set preferences — solo restore, team adventure, leadership intensive.
  3. FulfillCurated exclusive inventory + concierge; booked and reported like any benefit.

Product · Retreat Studio

Retreats that feel like a benefit — not a logistics project.

For employers

  • Budget bands per seat / team / annual pool
  • Policy templates: leadership, high performers, all-hands
  • Outcome tags: retention, burnout reset, alignment
  • One invoice, one dashboard, ROI for finance

For employees & teams

  • Preference quiz → shortlist of formats
  • Wellness, adventure, creative, culinary, quiet focus
  • Inclusive by design
  • Book as a team or claim a personal reset
